ShieldBuild Documentation

OSHA compliance tracking and safety management for construction crews.

Overview

ShieldBuild is a flat-rate OSHA compliance tracker designed for construction safety managers. It replaces per-user pricing models with predictable monthly billing tied to company size rather than headcount. The platform centralizes daily site logs, inspection checklists, incident reporting, and audit-ready documentation in a single interface.

Core capabilities include:

  • Daily Site Logs: Foremen log site conditions, crew attendance, and safety observations in under two minutes.
  • Inspection Checklists: Pre-built OSHA-aligned templates for scaffolding, electrical, excavation, fall protection, and PPE.
  • Incident Reporting: Structured incident and near-miss reports with photo attachments and root-cause analysis fields.
  • OSHA 300 Log Generation: Auto-populate injury/illness logs from incident reports (Phase 2).
  • Compliance Calendar: Track training expirations, permit renewals, and inspection deadlines (Phase 2).

ShieldBuild also supports healthcare construction projects requiring ICRA (Infection Control Risk Assessment) compliance alongside standard OSHA regulations.

Getting Started

To begin using ShieldBuild:

  1. Select a plan based on crew size and compliance needs (Crew, Builder, or Healthcare Constructor).
  2. Create your organization profile and add job sites.
  3. Invite foremen and safety officers via email; no per-user licensing is required.
  4. Configure notification preferences for inspection reminders and incident alerts.
  5. Begin logging daily site conditions or run an initial baseline inspection.

All plans include access to core inspection templates, incident reporting, and PDF/CSV exports. Advanced features like OSHA 300 log generation and multi-site support are available on higher tiers.

Templates

ShieldBuild includes pre-built, OSHA-aligned inspection checklists covering high-risk construction activities:

  • Scaffolding & Fall Protection
  • Electrical Safety & Lockout/Tagout
  • Excavation & Trenching
  • PPE Compliance
  • Hot Work Permits
  • Confined Space Entry

The Healthcare Constructor tier adds specialized ICRA templates:

  • ICRA Risk Assessment (Class I–IV)
  • Dust Containment Verification
  • Negative Pressure Monitoring Logs
  • Infection Control Permit Tracking

All templates are customizable. Changes are versioned automatically and can be reverted at any time.

Custom Policies

Safety managers can create custom inspection policies tailored to specific job hazards or client requirements:

  1. Navigate to Settings → Custom Policies.
  2. Define policy name, scope (site-wide or project-specific), and applicable trades.
  3. Add checklist items with pass/fail criteria, photo requirements, and mandatory notes.
  4. Assign approval workflows (self-certify vs. supervisor review).
  5. Set recurrence schedules (daily, weekly, milestone-based).

Custom policies integrate with incident reporting: failed checklist items can trigger immediate incident drafts with pre-populated context.

Export & Sharing

ShieldBuild supports multiple export formats for audits, client reporting, and internal records:

  • PDF Reports: Formatted inspection summaries, incident narratives, and photo evidence.
  • CSV/Excel: Raw data exports for trend analysis or integration with external BI tools.
  • OSHA 300/300A Logs: Auto-generated injury/illness summaries with DART rate calculations (Builder+ tiers, Phase 2).
  • Audit Packages: Bundled exports including signed certifications, training records, and permit histories (Healthcare tier includes BAA templates).

Exports can be scheduled automatically (e.g., weekly site summaries to project managers) or generated on-demand. Shared links expire after 30 days unless configured otherwise.

Version Control

All compliance artifacts in ShieldBuild are versioned:

  • Checklist Edits: Every modification to a template or custom policy creates a new revision. Previous versions are viewable and restorable.
  • Incident Reports: Updates to incident narratives, root-cause fields, or attached media are tracked with timestamps and editor identity.
  • Site Logs: Daily entries cannot be deleted but can be amended with correction notes linked to the original record.

Version history satisfies audit trail requirements for OSHA inspections and healthcare facility accreditation reviews. Retention defaults to 7 years but is configurable per organization.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues

  • Photos not uploading: Verify file size is under 10MB and format is JPG/PNG. Check plan photo quota if on Crew tier.
  • Missing checklist items: Ensure the correct template version is assigned to the site. Custom policy overrides may hide default items.
  • Export formatting errors: Clear browser cache and retry. If persistent, export as CSV and reformat externally.
  • Notification delays: Confirm email/SMS preferences in User Settings. Enterprise firewalls may block webhook callbacks; whitelist *.edgeiqlabs.com.
