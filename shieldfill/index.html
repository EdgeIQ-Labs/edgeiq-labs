Why Security Questionnaires Kill Deals

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI auto-fill?
Average fill rate is 80% across standard security questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ, VSA). Every AI-generated answer is flagged with a confidence score and reviewed by a human security analyst before delivery. You always have final approval.
What formats do you accept?
Starter plans handle Excel (.xlsx) in/out, which covers ~90% of vendor questionnaires. Growth and Enterprise plans add Word (.docx), PDF, and direct portal submission support.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. All KB data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We sign NDAs and BAAs as needed. Your security documentation never leaves our isolated processing environment. We're happy to complete your vendor assessment too.
How long does onboarding take?
Most teams are operational within 3–5 business days. Send us past questionnaires, security policies, SOC 2 reports, and architecture docs. We index everything and confirm readiness before your first live questionnaire.
Can this replace our security team?
No — and it shouldn't. ShieldFill handles the repetitive drafting work so your security team focuses on strategy, incident response, and actual risk reduction. Think of us as force multiplication, not replacement.
