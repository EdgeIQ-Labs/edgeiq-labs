Overview

PhishSim sends realistic phishing test emails to employees, tracks click rates, and provides interactive training modules for those who fail. Unlike traditional platforms that shame users, PhishSim focuses on education and positive reinforcement to build lasting security habits.

  • Realistic phishing email templates with customizable payloads
  • Automated campaign scheduling and department targeting
  • Interactive training modules triggered on failed tests
  • Detailed reporting with improvement trend tracking
  • REST API for integration with HRIS and LMS systems

Setup

Domain Verification

Before launching campaigns, verify your sending domain to ensure deliverability:

  1. Navigate to Account → Sending Profiles in the dashboard
  2. Add your SMTP server details (host, port, credentials)
  3. Configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records for your sending domain
  4. Send a test email to verify configuration
Use a dedicated subdomain (e.g., training.company.com) for phishing simulations to protect your primary domain reputation.

Compliance

Data Privacy

PhishSim is designed with privacy-by-default principles:

  • Credential Handling: Captured passwords are hashed immediately — plaintext never stored
  • Data Retention: Results auto-purge after 12 months (configurable)
  • Encryption: All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with audit logging

Employee Consent

Best practices for ethical phishing simulations:

  1. Prior Notification: Inform employees they will receive security tests (no specific dates)
  2. Opt-Out Process: Provide mechanism for employees to decline participation
  3. Union/Legal Review: Consult legal counsel and union reps before deployment
  4. No Punitive Measures: Failed tests trigger training, not disciplinary action
  5. Transparency Reports: Share aggregate results with employee representatives
PhishSim includes built-in consent tracking and opt-out management. Enable in Account → Compliance Settings.

Troubleshooting

Email Delivery Issues

Emails going to spam: Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC records. Use dedicated IP for high-volume sending. Warm up new domains gradually.

SMTP connection failures: Check firewall rules allow outbound port 25/587. Verify credentials and TLS settings. Test with telnet smtp.host.com 587.

Bounce backs: Clean target lists regularly. Remove invalid addresses. Monitor bounce rates — above 5% indicates list quality issues.

Low Engagement

Open rates below 20%: Subject lines may be filtered. Test different sender names. Check if emails are being quarantined by gateway.

Click rates below 5%: Templates may be too obvious. Increase realism with personalization. Time sends for when employees are busiest.

No credential submissions: Landing page may look suspicious. Test across devices/browsers. Ensure HTTPS certificate is valid.

Dashboard Access

Invalid Customer ID: Verify ID starts with ps_. Check for typos. Contact support if subscription is active but ID rejected.

Instance unreachable: Wait 5 minutes for provisioning. Check status at edgeiqlabs.com/account. Contact support if outage persists >15 minutes.
