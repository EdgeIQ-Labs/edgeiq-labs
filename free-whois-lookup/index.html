Frequently asked questions

What is WHOIS?
WHOIS is a protocol for querying domain registration databases. It returns information about who registered a domain, when it was created, when it expires, which registrar manages it, and what nameservers it uses. This tool uses RDAP (Registration Data Access Protocol), the modern replacement for WHOIS that returns structured JSON data instead of unformatted text.
Why should I monitor domain expiry dates?
When a domain expires, your website goes offline immediately, email stops working, and SSL certificates become invalid. Worse, attackers actively monitor expired domains and register them to impersonate your business, intercept email, or serve malware to your former visitors. Setting up expiry monitoring prevents accidental outages and domain hijacking.
Why doesn't this show the registrant name?
Since GDPR took effect in 2018, most registrars redact personal registrant data from public WHOIS/RDAP responses. The registrant name, email, and address are typically hidden unless the domain owner has opted out of privacy protection. Law enforcement and trademark holders can access full data through registrar abuse contacts.
What TLDs are supported?
This tool supports all major TLDs including .com, .net, .org, .io, .co, .dev, .app, .xyz, .me, .info, .us, .uk, .ca, .au, .de, and .fr. For less common TLDs, it falls back to the IANA RDAP bootstrap service which covers virtually all registered TLDs.
