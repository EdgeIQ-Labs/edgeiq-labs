Frequently Asked Questions

How does restore testing work?
ShieldVerify connects to your backup storage (S3, Azure Blob, local NAS), pulls a randomized sample of backup files, restores them to an isolated sandbox environment, and validates file integrity via SHA-256 checksums. The sandbox is destroyed after each test.
What backup types are supported?
We support Veeam, Acronis, AWS Backup, Azure Backup, Datto, and any S3-compatible object storage. Custom integrations available on Pro tier via API.
Is my backup data safe during testing?
Yes. Restore tests run in ephemeral sandboxes that are destroyed immediately after verification. We never store your backup data — only metadata about the test results (checksums, timestamps, pass/fail status).
What is the ransomware resilience score?
Our proprietary score (0-100) evaluates backup isolation from production networks, immutability settings, encryption at rest, MFA on backup consoles, recovery time objectives, and frequency of successful restore tests. Higher scores indicate better protection against ransomware encryption of backups.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled before the next billing cycle with no penalties. Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation then deleted.
