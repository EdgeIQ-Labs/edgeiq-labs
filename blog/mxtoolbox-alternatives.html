MXToolbox Alternatives in 2026: 5 Tools That Do More Than One-Off Checks

By EdgeIQ Labs May 20, 2026 7 min read

MXToolbox is great at what it does — instant, free lookups for DMARC, SPF, MX, blacklists, and DNS. If you need to quickly diagnose a problem, it's still the fastest option in 2026.

But its free tools are diagnostic, not preventive. You have to remember to check. You won't know your DMARC record was accidentally weakened, your SSL certificate is expiring in 11 days, or a new subdomain has been quietly added — unless you go back and run the check again.

The tools below are what you use when you want to stop manually checking and start being told when something changes. Most of them are free to start.

Disclosure: EdgeIQ Labs is on this list. We've included competitors where they're genuinely stronger for specific use cases.

What most people are actually looking for

When people search for "MXToolbox alternatives," they usually want one of three things:

  • Continuous monitoring instead of manual checks — set it up once and get alerted when things change
  • Broader coverage — not just email, but SSL, open ports, subdomains, DNS changes
  • Paid monitoring without MXToolbox's $129+/mo Delivery Center pricing — a more affordable option for small teams

Each alternative below is good at at least one of those things.

The 5 Best MXToolbox Alternatives

Alternative #1
EdgeIQ Labs
⭐ Best for continuous monitoring
Free (1 domain, weekly digest) · $9–$19/mo Pro
EdgeIQ is the most direct replacement for the "I want to stop manually checking" use case. You register your domain once and EdgeIQ monitors it automatically — DMARC, SPF, DKIM, SSL certificates, open ports, subdomains, DNS records — and sends you a weekly plain-English digest with everything that changed. Alternative #2
dmarcian
Best for DMARC forensics
Free trial · $19–$39/mo entry paid · scales with volume/domains
dmarcian is specifically for organizations that need to understand their DMARC aggregate reports in depth. It parses your rua= reports, identifies which sending sources are failing DMARC alignment, and lets you tag them. If you have multiple ESPs, CRMs, and transactional mail services and you need to get all of them into DMARC alignment before enforcing p=reject, dmarcian's source analysis is significantly more powerful than MXToolbox's Delivery Center offering.
  • Full rua/ruf forensic DMARC report analysis
  • Source tagging and compliance visualization
  • BIMI management
  • Dedicated DMARC expertise and community
Best for: Email professionals and enterprises with complex sending ecosystems who need forensic DMARC visibility.
Alternative #3
EasyDMARC
Best all-in-one DMARC platform
Free tier (limited) · ~$24/mo entry paid tier
EasyDMARC sits in the middle — more powerful than a basic monitoring tool, simpler than dmarcian. It offers a clean DMARC dashboard, aggregate report visualization, a guided implementation path to p=reject, and hosted SPF records (which solve the 10-lookup limit problem). If you want a dedicated DMARC platform with a good UI and don't need MXToolbox's breadth of diagnostic tools, EasyDMARC is a solid choice.
  • DMARC aggregate report analysis with source identification
  • Hosted SPF flattening (no more lookup limit issues)
  • Guided p=none → p=reject implementation wizard
  • Clean, well-designed dashboard
Best for: Teams that want an all-in-one DMARC management platform with a good UI, at a reasonable price.
Alternative #4
Postmark DMARC Digests
Free forever
Free — always
If you specifically need MXToolbox's DMARC aggregate report visibility and can't pay for anything, Postmark DMARC Digests is the free alternative. You set your DMARC rua= tag to their address, and they email you a weekly digest of your aggregate report data. No dashboard, no continuous monitoring, but it gives you weekly visibility into which sources are sending on behalf of your domain without any cost.
  • Completely free, forever
  • Weekly rua aggregate report digest
  • No dashboard or account required
Best for: Organizations on zero budget who want basic rua visibility. Note: this doesn't monitor or alert on record changes.
Alternative #5
SecurityTrails
Best for DNS + subdomain recon
Free tier (limited queries) · $50+/mo paid plans
SecurityTrails is the MXToolbox alternative for the DNS lookup and subdomain discovery use case. Where MXToolbox offers DNS diagnostic tools, SecurityTrails has a deep database of historical DNS records, subdomain enumeration, certificate transparency data, and IP/ASN intelligence. It's more of a threat intelligence and recon tool than a monitoring tool — but if you need to understand what DNS history looks like for a domain or enumerate subdomains, it's significantly more powerful than MXToolbox.
  • Historical DNS records database
  • Deep subdomain enumeration
  • IP/ASN and certificate transparency intelligence
  • API access for security automation
Best for: Security researchers, pentesters, and threat intelligence teams who need historical DNS and subdomain data.
