Overview

Lead Researcher aggregates publicly available business contact information from government registries, professional directories, and open corporate databases. Unlike scraping-based tools, we only index data that organizations have intentionally published for business outreach purposes. Every record includes source attribution and opt-out status verification.

Data Sources

  • SEC EDGAR: Public company filings with officer contact details
  • State Business Registries: LLC/corp registration records (all 50 states)
  • Professional Licensing Boards: Licensed practitioners with public practice addresses
  • Chamber of Commerce Directories: Member businesses with published contact info
  • Government Vendor Portals: Registered contractors and grant recipients
  • Open Corporate APIs: Companies House (UK), EU Business Registers

All sources are re-indexed weekly. Stale records (>180 days unverified) are automatically archived.

Compliance Notes

CAN-SPAM Compliance

All exported contacts include a valid physical postal address as required by 15 U.S.C. § 7704. Our system automatically appends unsubscribe links to email templates generated via the CLI. We maintain a real-time suppression list synced daily with DMA and FTC registries.

GDPR Compliance

EU/EEA contacts are only included when legitimate interest is documented (B2B context + published business contact). Each record contains a legal_basis field. Data subjects can request deletion via elr gdpr delete --email user@example.com, processed within 72 hours.

We do not scrape social media profiles, personal emails, or non-business directories. All data is sourced from official publications intended for commercial communication.

Troubleshooting

Empty search results? Broaden filters or remove --verified-only. New businesses may take 7-14 days to appear in public registries.

Rate limited? Free tier allows 100 requests/hour. Upgrade to $9/mo for 10k/hour. Use --batch flag for large exports to avoid throttling.

Outdated contact info? Run elr verify --email user@example.com to trigger real-time validation against MX records and SMTP handshake.

Missing EU contacts? Ensure --gdpr-legitimate-interest flag is set. Without it, EU records are excluded by default.
