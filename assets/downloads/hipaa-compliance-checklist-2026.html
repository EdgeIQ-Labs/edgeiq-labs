🏥 HIPAA Compliance Checklist 2026

EdgeIQ Labs · Security Rule Gap Assessment · v2026.1

This checklist covers the three pillars of HIPAA compliance: Administrative Safeguards, Physical Safeguards, and Technical Safeguards as defined in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.

Administrative Safeguards (§164.308)

Required Risk Analysis & Management — Conduct annual risk assessment of ePHI systems. Document threats, vulnerabilities, and remediation plan.
Required Security Personnel — Designate a privacy officer and security officer with documented responsibilities.
Required Workforce Training — Annual HIPAA training for all staff with access to PHI. Document attendance and comprehension.
Standard Access Authorization — Role-based access controls. Written procedures for granting, reviewing, and revoking access.
Standard Contingency Plan — Data backup, disaster recovery, and emergency mode operations. Test annually.
Required Breach Notification — Process to detect, investigate, and notify affected individuals within 60 days of discovery.
Addressable Business Associate Agreements — BAAs with all vendors handling PHI. Review and update annually.

Physical Safeguards (§164.310)

Required Facility Access Controls — Limit physical access to facilities housing ePHI. Visitor logs, badge access, or equivalent.
Standard Workstation Use & Security — Policies governing workstation positioning and screen visibility. Auto-lock after inactivity.
Required Device & Media Controls — Inventory of hardware/media containing ePHI. Secure disposal and reuse procedures.

Technical Safeguards (§164.312)

Required Access Control — Unique user IDs, emergency access procedures, automatic logoff, encryption/decryption.
Required Audit Controls — Logging of all access to ePHI. Regular review of audit logs for anomalous activity.
Standard Integrity Controls — Mechanisms to ensure ePHI is not improperly altered or destroyed. Checksums, digital signatures.
Required Transmission Security — Encryption of ePHI in transit (TLS 1.2+). Integrity controls for transmitted data.
Addressable Authentication — Multi-factor authentication for remote access to ePHI systems.

Documentation Requirements

  • All policies and procedures must be retained for 6 years from creation or last effective date
  • Risk assessments must be updated whenever environmental or operational changes occur
  • Incident response documentation must include timeline, affected records, and mitigation steps
  • Training records must include dates, topics covered, and attendee signatures
