TL;DR — The Quick Verdict

Valimail is a purpose-built enterprise DMARC platform. Its flagship Enforce product automates the path to p=reject — it processes your DMARC rua reports, identifies and categorizes every sending source automatically, and manages the enforcement transition without requiring manual DNS record editing. For a Fortune 500 company with hundreds of sending services, multiple brands, and a large security team, Valimail can save months of DMARC migration work. The price reflects that: Valimail is an enterprise contract, typically sold through a sales process.

EdgeIQ Inbox Shield approaches DMARC from the other direction. Rather than managing your DMARC record for you, it monitors your existing configuration continuously and alerts you the moment anything changes. No hosted DNS management, no automated source discovery at scale — just reliable monitoring, instant alerts, and a guided path to enforcement, at $9/month. Combined with Pulse Pro for SSL/header/subdomain monitoring and BrandGuard for lookalike domain detection, the complete EdgeIQ stack covers everything a small or mid-size business needs at a price Valimail can't touch.

The reality is that Valimail and EdgeIQ target completely different markets. If you're evaluating both, the right question isn't which is better — it's whether you're a large enterprise that needs automated DMARC enforcement management, or a smaller organization that needs reliable monitoring and alerting without enterprise overhead.

Bottom line: Valimail for enterprise-scale automated DMARC enforcement with dedicated support. EdgeIQ Inbox Shield for affordable, reliable DMARC + full security posture monitoring — built for businesses that don't have a dedicated email security engineer.

What Each Product Actually Does

Valimail

Valimail's core product is DMARC enforcement automation. Instead of manually reading through rua XML reports and figuring out which sending services are failing DMARC alignment, Valimail ingests your reports and automatically identifies sending sources — tagging known services (Salesforce, Marketo, HubSpot, etc.) and flagging unknown senders. It then manages your DMARC record automatically, safely stepping up enforcement only when it's confident all legitimate senders are aligned.

This "set it and walk away" approach is valuable for large organizations where the alternative is months of manual DNS work across a sprawling email ecosystem. Valimail also offers DMARC monitoring, reporting dashboards for compliance teams, and professional services for complex deployments. It's enterprise software with enterprise support expectations.

EdgeIQ Inbox Shield

Inbox Shield monitors your DMARC, SPF, and DKIM records continuously and sends you an immediate alert if anything changes. Policy weakened from p=reject to p=none? Alert. SPF record modified? Alert. DKIM key rotated or selector removed? Alert. You get a real-time A–F email authentication grade, plain-English explanations of what each check means, and a step-by-step guide toward enforcement.

Inbox Shield is part of the EdgeIQ platform — which means email authentication monitoring shares a dashboard and billing with SSL certificate monitoring, HTTP security header checks, subdomain discovery, open port scanning, and brand impersonation detection. For a business that's trying to understand and maintain its complete security posture (not just email), this consolidated view is significantly more practical than running five separate tools.

Are an SMB, startup, or mid-market company. Want self-serve, no sales process. Need DMARC monitoring plus SSL, headers, and brand protection. Are already at p=reject and want to stay that way. Are an MSP managing multiple client domains affordably.

Choose Valimail if you…

Are a large enterprise with dozens of sending services across multiple brands. Need fully automated DMARC enforcement management without manual DNS work. Have a dedicated email security team and budget. Are managing DMARC compliance across a complex multi-brand organization.

Already at p=reject? You may not need Valimail anymore

Valimail's highest-value proposition is the automated migration from p=none to p=reject — figuring out every sending source and ensuring they're all properly authenticated before you enforce. Once you've achieved enforcement, the ongoing value is mainly monitoring and alerting: "did anything slip back to p=none, did a new rogue sender appear?" That's exactly what EdgeIQ Inbox Shield does, continuously, for $9/month. Many teams renewing Valimail contracts after achieving enforcement could switch to a simpler monitoring tool and reinvest the savings.

FAQ

What's the best Valimail alternative for small businesses?
For SMBs, the most practical Valimail alternative is a combination of EdgeIQ Inbox Shield (DMARC/SPF/DKIM monitoring and alerts) and Pulse Pro (SSL, header, subdomain monitoring). This covers your complete security posture at under $30/month — without a sales call, contract, or enterprise onboarding process.
Can I migrate from Valimail to EdgeIQ?
If you achieved DMARC enforcement with Valimail and want to switch to a simpler monitoring tool, the process is straightforward: ensure your DMARC record is set to p=reject in your own DNS (if Valimail was managing a hosted DMARC record, you'll need to migrate it back to your own DNS), then set up EdgeIQ Inbox Shield to monitor the record going forward. EdgeIQ will alert you immediately if the policy ever weakens.
Does EdgeIQ do automated DMARC source management like Valimail?
No. EdgeIQ Inbox Shield monitors your DMARC record and alerts you to changes. It does not process rua aggregate reports to auto-tag sending sources or manage your DNS record for you. If you're in the middle of a large-scale DMARC deployment with many unknown sending services, Valimail's automation offers significant value that EdgeIQ doesn't replicate.
Is there a free version of EdgeIQ?
Yes. EdgeIQ has free tools that require no account: the domain security score gives you a full A–F grade for your domain in seconds, and the Inbox Shield instant check shows your current DMARC, SPF, and DKIM status. Paid monitoring with email alerts starts at $9/month, with a 14-day free trial.
