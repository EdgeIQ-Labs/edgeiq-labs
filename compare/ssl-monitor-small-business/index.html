When CertWatch Pro makes sense

If your only gap is certificate expiry visibility and you do not need uptime probes or transaction monitoring, paying for a general-purpose monitor adds cost without adding coverage. CertWatch Pro does one job daily and emails you while you still have weeks to act.

When a broader tool makes sense

If you also need HTTP uptime checks, keyword monitoring, or status pages, UptimeRobot or Pingdom may cover more ground in a single bill. The tradeoff is price and complexity: SSL becomes a feature inside a larger product rather than the product itself.
