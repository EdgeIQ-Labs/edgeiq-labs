Frequently asked questions

Why flat-rate instead of per-user pricing?

Construction crews fluctuate weekly — you might have 15 people on site Monday and 40 by Friday. Per-user pricing ($15–30/user/mo) makes budgeting impossible and punishes growth. Our flat rate covers your entire organization regardless of headcount.

Does ShieldBuild work offline?

Phase 1 requires an internet connection. Phase 2 will include a Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline-first data entry, background sync, and local caching so foremen can log inspections and incidents even without cell service on site.

What makes the Healthcare Constructor tier different?

Healthcare construction projects (hospital renovations, clinic buildouts) require ICRA (Infection Control Risk Assessment) compliance alongside standard OSHA regulations. The Healthcare Constructor tier includes ICRA risk assessment templates, dust containment verification checklists, negative pressure monitoring logs, infection control permit tracking, and HIPAA-adjacent worker safety protocols. No other construction compliance tool addresses this niche.

Can I import my existing OSHA 300 logs?

Yes. Upload your existing OSHA 300 spreadsheet (CSV or Excel) and we'll migrate historical incident data. This gives you trend analysis and year-over-year comparisons from day one.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No. All tiers are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. Annual billing with 20% discount will be available in Phase 2.

How does this relate to Compliance Shield?

Compliance Shield handles HIPAA data privacy and IT security for healthcare practices. ShieldBuild handles physical construction safety and OSHA compliance. They're complementary products for different buyers — but if you're doing healthcare construction, the Healthcare Constructor tier leverages our existing HIPAA expertise to cover ICRA and adjacent safety protocols.
