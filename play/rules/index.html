Community Rules

Our public Minecraft server is open to everyone. These rules keep it a safe, fair, and fun place to play.

1 Be Respectful

  • Treat all players with respect. No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
  • No discrimination based on race, gender, nationality, religion, or any other characteristic.
  • Keep chat friendly — this server is accessible to players of all ages.

2 No Cheating or Exploiting

  • No hacked clients, X-ray texture packs, auto-clickers, or unfair mods.
  • Do not exploit bugs or glitches. Report them to support@edgeiqlabs.com instead.
  • Approved mods: Optifine, Sodium, Iris, minimaps (no entity radar), performance mods.

3 No Griefing or Stealing

  • Do not destroy or modify another player's builds without permission.
  • Do not steal from other players' chests, farms, or storage.
  • Unclaimed land may be built on — claim your area to protect it.

4 No Spamming or Advertising

  • Do not spam the chat with repeated messages or symbols.
  • Do not advertise other servers, Discord servers, or unrelated websites in chat.

5 Keep It Appropriate

  • No inappropriate builds, signs, or usernames.
  • No discussion of illegal activities.
  • Maintain a PG-13 standard in all public chat.

6 Consequences

  • Rule violations may result in a warning, temporary mute/ban, or permanent ban depending on severity.
  • Ban appeals can be sent to support@edgeiqlabs.com.
  • Staff decisions are final. Arguing with staff in chat is not permitted.
