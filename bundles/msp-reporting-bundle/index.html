White-label security reports for your clients.

Package Pulse Pro + CertWatch Pro + UptimePing Pro into branded monthly reports you send under your own name. No extra tooling, no per-client dashboards to manage. One bundle, one price.

Your clients want proof that their infrastructure is watched. They do not want another login. This bundle gives you the raw monitoring data from three focused tools and lets you wrap it in your own branding before it hits their inbox.

What is included

Pulse Pro

Weekly domain security scans covering SSL, headers, DNS, subdomains, and open ports. Email digest when something changes.

CertWatch Pro

Daily SSL certificate expiry checks with email alerts at 30, 14, 7, and 1 day before expiry.

UptimePing Pro

5-minute uptime checks with response time tracking and instant downtime alerts.

No single checkout. Each product has its own subscription and can be cancelled independently. See pricing for current rates and trials.

How MSPs use this

  • Run weekly scans across client domains from your own accounts.
  • Export results or forward digests to your branded email address.
  • Add your logo and commentary before sending to the client.
  • Use CertWatch alerts as early-warning tickets in your own helpdesk.

Who this is for

  • MSPs managing 5–50 client domains who need recurring evidence of monitoring.
  • IT agencies that already handle patching but lack a lightweight security report.
  • Solo consultants who want to offer monitoring without building a dashboard.
