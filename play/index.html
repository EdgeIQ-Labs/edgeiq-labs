The Starter plan includes a 3-day free trial via Stripe. You'll enter your card at signup but won't be charged until day 4. Cancel before then and you owe nothing.

You get full access to a Pterodactyl web panel — start/stop, live console, file manager, and SFTP file access. No command line knowledge required.

Can I switch games after signing up?

Yes. Email us at support@edgeiqlabs.com and we'll reinstall the server with the game of your choice.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes. Email us and we'll migrate your server data and move you to the new plan.

Where are the servers located?

Our servers run on dedicated Dell PowerEdge hardware in the northeastern United States.

Is there a contract?

None. Month-to-month billing. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.
