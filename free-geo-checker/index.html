    What makes a website visible to AI search engines?

    Traditional SEO focused on keywords and backlinks. AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) work differently — they parse your page structure, extract entities, and look for citable passages. Here's what they check:

    JSON-LD Structured Data
    Machine-readable blocks that tell AI engines exactly what your page is about — your business type, FAQs, articles, products. Without it, AI has to guess.
    FAQPage Schema
    The single highest-value signal for AI citations. When you mark up Q&A pairs with FAQPage schema, AI engines directly quote your answers in generated responses.
    Entity Markup
    Organization, LocalBusiness, or Person schema that anchors your brand in AI knowledge graphs. Without it, AI can't reliably attribute information to your company.
    AI Bot Access (robots.txt)
    If your robots.txt blocks GPTBot, CCBot, or PerplexityBot, your content will never appear in AI-generated answers. Many sites accidentally block all AI crawlers.
    Semantic HTML
    Using <main>, <article>, <nav>, and proper heading hierarchy helps AI parsers extract the right content instead of navigation menus and footers.
    Content Depth
    AI engines need substantive text to find citable passages. Pages under 300 words rarely get referenced because there isn't enough signal to extract.

    GEO vs traditional SEO — what changed?

    Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) doesn't replace SEO — it adds a new layer. Traditional search engines rank links. AI search engines synthesize answers by extracting facts from multiple sources. To become one of those sources:

    • Structured data beats keyword density. AI engines parse JSON-LD first. A page with perfect FAQ schema outranks a keyword-stuffed page every time in AI citations.
    • Entity clarity matters more than domain authority. AI needs to know WHO you are (Organization schema) before it trusts WHAT you say.
    • Blocking AI bots is self-sabotage. Many site owners blocked GPTBot thinking it protects their content. In reality, it just makes them invisible to 100M+ ChatGPT users.
    • Citable passages win. Write clear, factual paragraphs that answer specific questions. AI extracts these verbatim when generating responses.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
    GEO is the practice of optimizing your website so AI-powered search engines — like ChatGPT's web search, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews — can discover, understand, and cite your content. Unlike traditional SEO which focuses on ranking blue links, GEO focuses on structured data, entity clarity, semantic HTML, and content depth so AI systems can extract and reference your information directly in generated answers.
    Why doesn't my website show up in ChatGPT or Perplexity answers?
    The most common reasons: (1) No JSON-LD structured data — AI engines can't identify what your page is about. (2) Missing FAQPage schema — you have no citable Q&A pairs. (3) robots.txt blocks AI bots like GPTBot or PerplexityBot. (4) Thin content under 300 words with no substantive passages to extract. (5) No Organization schema so AI can't attribute facts to your brand. This checker tests all five.
    Should I block AI bots in my robots.txt?
    Only if you explicitly don't want your content used in AI training or responses. Blocking GPTBot, CCBot, PerplexityBot, etc. means your pages will NOT appear in AI-generated answers. For most businesses, this is counterproductive — being cited by AI is now a primary traffic and authority channel. Allow AI bot access unless you have a specific reason to block it.
    How do I add FAQPage schema to my site?
    Add a JSON-LD script block to your page's <head> with @type set to FAQPage, containing an array of Question objects each with acceptedAnswer. Most CMS platforms (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify) have plugins or built-in fields for this. After adding, re-run this checker to verify it's detected correctly.
