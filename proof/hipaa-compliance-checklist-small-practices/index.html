HIPAA compliance without the enterprise price tag.

Small healthcare practices face the same regulatory requirements as hospital systems, with a fraction of the staff and budget. This checklist covers the controls that matter most when you have fewer than 20 employees.

The checklist

  • Risk assessment: Document where PHI lives, who touches it, and what could go wrong. Update annually or after any system change.
  • Access controls: Unique user IDs for every staff member. Role-based access so front desk cannot see clinical notes. MFA on any system holding PHI.
  • Audit logging: Enable logging on EHR, email, and file storage. Review logs monthly or automate alerts for unusual access patterns.
  • Encryption: Encrypt PHI at rest and in transit. Full-disk encryption on laptops. TLS 1.2+ for web portals and email.
  • BAA management: Signed Business Associate Agreements with every vendor that touches PHI. Track renewal dates. Use Evidence Collector to automate evidence gathering.
  • Incident response plan: Written procedure for breach detection, containment, notification, and documentation. Test annually with a tabletop exercise.
  • Employee training: Annual HIPAA awareness training with documented completion. Include phishing recognition and password hygiene.
This checklist is a starting point, not legal advice. Consult a HIPAA attorney or compliance consultant for your specific situation.
