How to use it

  1. Run the free report first. It shows a watermarked preview and your top three findings, so you can see whether a full report is worth buying.
  2. Buy the full $49 report when you need the complete finding list and remediation steps. Work through it in order of risk, not in order of how easy each item is.
  3. Set up the Evidence Collector and give each person their forms. Uploads land against a control, so a signed policy acknowledgment sits next to the item it proves.
  4. Export the ZIP when someone asks for it. The dated uploads are already grouped, which is the part that saves the most time.

Who this is for

  • A dental, optometry, chiropractic, or therapy practice preparing for an audit
  • An office manager who is the whole compliance team
  • A practice that has policies in a drawer but no dated evidence folder
  • Anyone who has been asked for a HIPAA risk analysis and does not know where it lives

Start with the HIPAA checklist for small practices to see the items and the evidence to keep for each. The older HIPAA checklist article covers the same ground in more depth.

What it does not cover

Neither tool gives legal advice, signs a BAA on your behalf, or certifies your practice. Your risk analysis and the decisions that follow are yours. The tools keep the work organized. FAQ

What does the Compliance Evidence Kit include?
Two separately purchased tools: the Compliance Report Generator at $49 for a one-time full report, and the Compliance Evidence Collector at $99 per month for ongoing evidence uploads and ZIP export.
Is the $49 report a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase. The free tier shows a watermarked report with the top three findings; the $49 version unlocks full findings, remediation steps, and an unwatermarked report.
Can I use the Evidence Collector on its own?
Yes. The collector handles form-based uploads, control tagging, a manager dashboard, and a one-click ZIP export. The report is useful when you need a point-in-time grade and a remediation list first.
Does this make my practice HIPAA compliant?
No tool can make that claim for you. These products organize findings and evidence for HIPAA, OSHA, SOC 2, and PCI-DSS controls. Your risk analysis and your own counsel or consultant still decide what compliance means for your practice.
