Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?
Pulse, Inbox Shield, Vendor Watch, and Compliance all have free tiers that never expire. You can use the core scanning features indefinitely at no cost. Paid plans add automated scheduling, email digests, and alerts.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. All paid plans are month-to-month with no contracts. You can cancel from your account page at any time — your access continues until the end of the billing period.
Does one subscription cover multiple domains?
Each subscription covers one domain or tenant. If you need to monitor multiple domains you can add additional subscriptions. MSP Essentials covers unlimited client domains under one plan.
What is the Workspace Posture early access rate?
Early access subscribers pay $19/mo, which is locked in forever — standard pricing will be $39/mo. The rate never increases for active early access accounts.
Do you offer annual billing?
Annual billing is not yet available but is on the roadmap. Contact support@edgeiqlabs.com if you'd like to discuss annual pricing.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. No PayPal or invoice billing currently available.
