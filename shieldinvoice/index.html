Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI extraction?
ShieldInvoice returns a confidence score (0–1) with every extraction. For clean digital PDFs, accuracy typically exceeds 95%. Scanned or low-quality documents may score lower — the dashboard highlights these for manual review so nothing slips through unchecked.
Is my invoice data secure?
All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We process invoices in isolated Cloudflare Workers environments with no persistent storage beyond your account's KV namespace. We never share or sell your data. Full deletion is available on request.
What integrations are supported?
CSV export works with any spreadsheet or ERP system. QuickBooks Online export is available on the Pro plan. We're adding Xero and NetSuite connectors next. Email ingest works with any email provider — just forward to your unique address.
Can I process invoices from multiple vendors?
Yes. ShieldInvoice handles unlimited vendors across all plans. The AI automatically identifies and categorizes vendor names, and the dashboard shows top-vendor analytics so you can spot spending patterns.
What happens if I exceed my monthly limit?
On the Starter plan, you'll receive a notification at 80% usage. Additional invoices are billed at $1.50 each, or you can upgrade to Pro for unlimited processing mid-cycle with prorated billing.
Do you offer a free trial?
We offer a live demo with sample invoices so you can see extraction quality before subscribing. Contact billing@edgeiqlabs.com for a guided walkthrough tailored to your invoice formats.
