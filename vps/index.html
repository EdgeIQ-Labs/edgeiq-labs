FAQ

Common questions

What OS can I run?

We support Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, Debian 12, AlmaLinux 9, and Rocky Linux 9 out of the box. Need something else? Just ask — if it runs on LXC or KVM, we can likely accommodate it.

Do I get a real public IP address?

Yes. Every VPS gets a dedicated public IP address with SSH access. You can run web servers, game servers, VPNs, or anything else that needs inbound connections.

What kind of hardware is this running on?

Our VPS infrastructure runs on Dell PowerEdge servers with ZFS SSD storage. These are the same machines powering our game hosting — enterprise hardware, not cheap cloud VMs.

Can I install Docker or run a web server?

Yes to both. Full root access means you can install and run anything — Docker, Nginx, Apache, Node.js, Python, databases, or anything else you need.

How do I order?

Click any plan button and send us an email with your plan, preferred OS, and any special requirements. We'll have your VPS ready within 30 minutes during business hours.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

None. Month-to-month billing. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.

Where are the servers located?

US East Coast. Same data center as our game servers — low latency for North American users.
