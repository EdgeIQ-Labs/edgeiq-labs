FAQ

Common questions about the MSP plan.

Can I swap client domains?

Yes — you can add, remove, or swap any of your 10 client domains from your account dashboard at any time. There's no penalty for changing them.

Do I need a separate account per client?

No. All 10 client domains are managed under your single MSP account. One login, one dashboard, one Stripe bill.

What does "Pulse scan" cover?

Each weekly Pulse scan checks SSL certificate validity and expiry, HTTP security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options), subdomain enumeration, DNS records, and open port exposure.

What does "Inbox Shield" check?

Inbox Shield validates SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records for each domain and grades them A–F. It also checks for DMARC policy weaknesses that allow spoofing.

Who receives the weekly digests?

All digests go to the email address on your MSP account. You decide whether to forward them to clients or summarize findings yourself.

Can I use this for my own domain too?

Yes — any of the 10 seats can be used for your own domain. Mix client and internal domains freely.

What if I need more than 10 seats?

Email us at hello@edgeiqlabs.com and we can discuss a custom volume arrangement for larger MSPs.

How do I cancel?

From your account dashboard, click "Manage Billing" to reach the Stripe portal. Cancel with one click — no emails, no friction. Your seats stay active until the end of the billing period.
