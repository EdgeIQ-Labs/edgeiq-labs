FAQ

Common questions

Which games do you support?

Right now we support Minecraft Java Edition (Paper, Forge, and Fabric). We're actively adding more games — if you'd like to request one, email us at support@edgeiqlabs.com.

How does the free trial work?

The Starter plan includes a 3-day free trial via Stripe. You'll enter your card at signup but won't be charged until day 4. Cancel before then and you owe nothing.

How do I manage my server?

You get full access to a Pterodactyl web panel — start/stop the server, live console, file manager, and SFTP access. No command line knowledge required.

Is provisioning really automatic?

Yes — for Minecraft Java plans, your server is created automatically via our webhook system the moment your Stripe payment completes. You'll receive your panel login by email.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes. Email us and we'll migrate your world data and move you to the new plan.

Where are the servers?

Our servers run on dedicated Dell PowerEdge hardware located in the northeastern United States.

Is there a contract?

None. Month-to-month billing. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.
