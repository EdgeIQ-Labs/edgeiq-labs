FAQ

Common questions

Is this a VPS or a container?

It's an LXC container running on Proxmox. LXC shares the host kernel, which makes it lighter than a full VM but means you can't load custom kernel modules. For most web apps, bots, dev environments, and light workloads it works great. If you need full KVM virtualization, email us.

Do I get a dedicated public IP?

Not currently — we operate from a single public IP. Your container gets a dedicated range of ports forwarded to it for SSH and your apps. If your project specifically requires a dedicated IP, reach out and we'll discuss options.

What OS can I run?

Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, Debian 12, Rocky Linux 9, and AlmaLinux 9 are all available. Just specify which one you want when you order.

Can I run Docker?

Yes, with privileged mode enabled on the container. Let us know when you order and we'll configure it. Be aware that privileged containers have less isolation from the host.

How long does setup take?

Provisioning is fully automated — your container is created and SSH credentials emailed to you within minutes of payment. No waiting, no manual steps.

Is there a contract?

None. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime by emailing us.

Where are the servers?

Our hardware is located in the northeastern United States on a residential fiber connection. This is a small, owner-operated service — not a datacenter.
