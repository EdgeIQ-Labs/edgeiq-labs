Template: Incident Response Plan

🚨 Incident Response Procedure v1.0

Severity Levels:

  • P1 — Critical: Active breach, data exfiltration, ransomware. Response: 15 min SLA.
  • P2 — High: Unauthorized access detected, service outage affecting customers. Response: 1 hour.
  • P3 — Medium: Suspicious activity, failed auth spikes, misconfiguration. Response: 4 hours.
  • P4 — Low: Policy violation, minor anomaly. Response: next business day.

Response Steps: Detect → Contain → Eradicate → Recover → Post-mortem (within 5 business days)

Notification Thresholds: [Define based on regulatory requirements — HIPAA: 60 days, GDPR: 72 hours]
