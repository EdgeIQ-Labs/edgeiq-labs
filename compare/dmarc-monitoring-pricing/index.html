Why EdgeIQ focuses on weekly grades

A dashboard full of charts tells you what happened. A weekly grade tells you whether to do something about it. Inbox Shield sends a plain-English email every Monday with your SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status and a fix for anything that dropped. No login required unless you want the history.

When to look elsewhere

If you manage dozens of sending domains, need aggregate report parsing at volume, or are pursuing BIMI enforcement with legal sign-off, the enterprise-tier tools earn their price. For a small business checking its own domain and a few vendors, the entry tiers above cover the gap without the overhead.
