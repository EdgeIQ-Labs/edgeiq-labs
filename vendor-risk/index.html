Is this suitable for small healthcare practices?

267|

Yes. Vendor Risk is designed specifically for small healthcare practices (dental, therapy, chiropractic, optometry, med spa) and SMBs that need HIPAA-compliant vendor management without enterprise pricing or complexity.

Do I need Compliance Shield or PhishSim to use this?

271|

No. Vendor Risk works standalone. However, if you also use Compliance Shield or PhishSim, cross-product signals automatically enrich your vendor risk scores at no extra cost.

What happens when a BAA expires?

275|

You receive email alerts at 90, 30, and 7 days before expiry. Expired BAAs automatically downgrade the vendor's risk score and flag them in your audit-ready reports.

Can I import my existing vendor list?

279|

Yes. Upload a CSV with vendor names and domains. AI will automatically pull public evidence (SOC2, HITRUST, breach history) to pre-populate each vendor profile.

How does AI auto-fill work?

287|

Our AI scans vendor websites, public certification registries (SOC2, HITRUST, ISO), the HHS breach portal, and your organization's past assessments to pre-populate questionnaire responses. Low-confidence answers are flagged for human review.
