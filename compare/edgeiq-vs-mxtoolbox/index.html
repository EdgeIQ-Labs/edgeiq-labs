TL;DR — The Quick Verdict

MXToolbox is the go-to for one-off diagnostics. Need to look up a DMARC record right now? Check if an IP is blacklisted? Test your MX servers? MXToolbox has free tools that do exactly that — instantly, no account needed. It's been around since 2004 and every email deliverability engineer knows it.

EdgeIQ Labs is built around the question MXToolbox doesn't answer: what changed since last time I checked? EdgeIQ monitors your domain continuously — DMARC, SPF, DKIM, SSL certificates, subdomains, open ports, DNS — and sends you a plain-English alert when anything changes. You don't have to remember to check. The monitoring runs automatically.

Bottom line: If you need to diagnose a problem right now, use MXToolbox. If you want to be notified when something breaks before your customers notice, use EdgeIQ. Most serious teams use both — MXToolbox for troubleshooting, EdgeIQ for continuous oversight.

What Each Product Actually Does

MXToolbox

MXToolbox is a toolkit. It gives you 100+ individual lookup tools — DMARC lookup, SPF lookup, MX lookup, blacklist check, email header analyzer, SMTP diagnostics, and more. You type in a domain or IP, click a button, get the result. All free for basic lookups.

MXToolbox Delivery Center (their paid product) adds monitoring and alerts, but it's focused primarily on email deliverability — blacklist monitoring, DMARC reporting, and inbox placement testing. It's enterprise-oriented with pricing that starts around $129/mo and scales with volume.

EdgeIQ Labs

EdgeIQ starts with continuous monitoring across your entire external attack surface — not just email. Pulse monitors your SSL certificates, open ports, subdomains, DNS records, and CVEs. Inbox Shield monitors your DMARC, SPF, and DKIM records and alerts you when policy or records change. BrandGuard watches for new lookalike domains. All of it sends you a plain-English weekly digest (or daily on Pro), so the monitoring is zero-effort after setup.

Pricing starts free for one domain, with Pro plans at $9–$19/mo. There are no per-seat fees and no enterprise contracts.

Which One Is Right for You?

Choose EdgeIQ Labs if…

You want to be automatically notified when anything changes — SSL expiry, DMARC policy drift, new subdomain, open port — without logging in and manually checking. Set up once, get weekly digests.

Choose MXToolbox if…

You're debugging an email deliverability issue right now and need deep diagnostic tools — SMTP testing, MX lookup, email header analysis — with no account required.

Choose EdgeIQ Labs if…

You're a small business, startup, or solo IT owner who can't afford the time to check security posture manually. EdgeIQ's free plan covers one domain at zero cost.

Choose MXToolbox if…

You're a high-volume email sender who needs inbox placement testing, detailed DMARC XML report analysis, and dedicated deliverability support. MXToolbox Delivery Center is purpose-built for that.

Choose EdgeIQ Labs if…

You want attack surface monitoring beyond email — SSL certificates, open ports, subdomain discovery, lookalike domain alerts — all in one weekly digest.

Choose MXToolbox if…

You're an IT consultant or MSP who needs a quick toolbox for ad-hoc client diagnostics. MXToolbox's free lookup tools are unmatched for speed and depth.

The honest take: These tools largely don't compete. MXToolbox is a diagnostic tool; EdgeIQ is a monitoring tool. Most security-conscious teams bookmark both. If you only pick one and your goal is not being surprised by something breaking, EdgeIQ fits that job better at a significantly lower price point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can EdgeIQ Labs replace MXToolbox completely?
For monitoring and alerting, yes. For ad-hoc diagnostics — SMTP testing, email header analysis, one-off lookups — MXToolbox has a deeper toolbox. Most teams use EdgeIQ for continuous monitoring and MXToolbox when actively troubleshooting an issue.
Does EdgeIQ Labs offer DMARC XML report analysis?
Inbox Shield provides DMARC posture monitoring, policy alerts, and setup guidance. Deep forensic XML report parsing (volume breakdowns, source analysis) is more MXToolbox Delivery Center territory. If you need both, they complement each other.
Is MXToolbox free?
MXToolbox's individual lookup tools are free and unlimited. Their paid Delivery Center product (email monitoring, blacklist alerting, inbox placement) starts around $129/mo. EdgeIQ's free plan includes continuous monitoring for one domain with a weekly digest — no paid tier required to get started.
Does EdgeIQ Labs check blacklists?
Inbox Shield Pro includes blacklist monitoring for your domain. You can also use the free DMARC checker for a one-off email authentication check, or check out our blacklist guide for manual checking steps.
