Why prime rate matters

The prime rate is the benchmark interest rate that banks use to set rates on credit cards, home equity lines, and other variable-rate loans. When the Federal Reserve raises or lowers its target rate, the prime rate typically moves in lockstep within days.

Most variable APR credit cards are priced as prime rate + margin. A 0.25% prime rate increase means your card's APR increases by exactly 0.25%. On a $5,000 balance at 22% APR, that's an extra $12.50 per year in interest — compounding monthly.

Banks don't always notify you clearly when your rate changes. By the time you notice on your statement, you've already been paying the higher rate for weeks. Rate Watch gives you advance notice so you can adjust payments, transfer balances, or pay down debt before the higher rate bites.

Frequently asked questions

What is the prime rate?
The prime rate is the interest rate that commercial banks charge their most creditworthy customers. It's set by individual banks but closely tracks the Federal Reserve's federal funds rate. Most variable APR credit cards use prime rate plus a margin as their interest rate formula.
How does this work?
We monitor the Federal Reserve's H.15 statistical release every 6 hours for changes to the bank prime rate. When a change is detected, we immediately send an email alert to all subscribers with the new rate, the amount of change, and what it means for variable APR cards.
Is this financial advice?
No. Rate Watch is an informational tool only. We provide factual alerts about published interest rate changes. We do not recommend any financial products, strategies, or actions. Consult a qualified financial advisor for personal financial decisions.
How quickly will I get an alert?
Our system checks the Fed's published data every 6 hours. When a rate change is detected, emails are sent within minutes. The Fed typically publishes rate changes on the same day they take effect.
Can I unsubscribe?
Yes. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us to be removed. We never sell or share your email address.
